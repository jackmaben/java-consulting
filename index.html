Software Platforms We Have Built for Real Business Operations

Optimize Tech Studio has developed software for claims processing, employee recognition, security operations, field activity, reporting, and multi-role business workflows. These projects show how we translate operational requirements, system relationships, access rules, and manual processes into working software.

My Accident 24/7: Claims Management Platform

We built a web and mobile claims management platform that replaced paper forms, phone follow-ups, and disconnected claim records with one structured workflow. Claimants can report accidents, upload documents, track claim status, and receive updates, while administrators manage verification, reporting, and claim files from one system.

Result: Administrative work fell from approximately 3 hours to 20 minutes per claim, based on the client’s reported workflow.

Built with:FlutterReactNode.jsPostgreSQL
View Accident Claims Management Case Study →

Talent Prospecting: Positive Peer Recognition Platform

We developed a multi-role workforce platform for Positive Peer Recognition surveys. Company administrators, managers, and employees use separate workflows for team onboarding, surveys, peer nominations, automated reminders, and analytical reporting. The platform also uses shuffled peer selections and role-based access to support fairer talent assessment.

Business impact: The platform allows Positive Peer to run its recognition methodology across organisations of different sizes while turning peer nominations into structured talent insights.

Built with:PHP LaravelMySQLSSOCSV data import

Pulse Security: Security Operations Management System

We built a centralised operations platform that connects customers, sites, jobs, equipment, parts, assets, projects, compliance, and field activity. The system replaced fragmented operational tools with connected workflows, automated job costing, stage-based project controls, clearance management, and role-based access.

Business impact: Pulse Security now manages core operations from one platform, while automated VAT and job-cost calculations reduce manual work and pricing-error risk.

02 Process

Agile Sprint Cycles Built for Predictable Delivery

Our software development lifecycle follows a structured 6-stage delivery framework from discovery to support. We use Agile development, a clear software delivery process, DevOps workflow, and continuous stakeholder collaboration to ensure quality built into every phase, transparent project governance, and post-launch optimization support.

1Discovery

Discovery and Technical Assessment

We review business goals, existing systems, workflows, integrations, constraints, and technical risks before defining the delivery scope.

2Design

Architecture and Delivery Planning

We define system boundaries, integrations, data flows, technical dependencies, milestones, and the first development backlog before implementation begins.

3Development

Iterative Development

Our engineers build in 2-week sprint cycles, with working software reviewed regularly instead of delaying validation until the end of development.

4QA

Testing and Technical Review

We test functionality, integrations, security controls, performance, and critical business workflows before each approved release.

5Deploy

Deployment and Knowledge Transfer

We deploy the agreed release, transfer source code and technical assets, and document ownership across the delivered system.

6Support

Support and Continuous Improvement

After launch, we handle defects, planned enhancements, performance improvements, and product changes based on agreed support priorities.

05 Why us

Why Choose Optimize Tech Studio

Optimize Tech Studio combines custom software engineering, AI expertise, and practical delivery processes for businesses that need new systems, modernization, integrations, or AI capabilities without losing control of their existing technology.

We Improve Existing Systems Before Recommending Replacement

We assess what should be kept, integrated, modernized, or replaced before proposing development work. This helps avoid unnecessary rebuilds and keeps useful systems, data, and workflows in place where they still provide value.

Direct Access to Software and AI Engineers

Clients work directly with the engineers who make architecture decisions, build integrations, test critical workflows, and prepare each release for delivery. This reduces communication layers and keeps technical decisions connected to business requirements.

Working Software in Short Delivery Cycles

We deliver development through 2-week sprint cycles and use working software to validate requirements early. A first working demo can be available within two weeks, while focused PoC and MVP engagements typically run for 4–8 weeks.

Software Built Around Real Business Operations

Our team has delivered platforms for claims processing, employee recognition, security operations, enterprise workflows, and other operational use cases. We design software around users, business rules, permissions, integrations, and system relationships rather than isolated feature lists.

Clear Software Ownership

Clients retain 100% ownership of the source code and agreed project assets. We support knowledge transfer and technical documentation so businesses are not dependent on Optimize Tech Studio after delivery.

US Presence With India Engineering Delivery

Optimize Tech Studio combines US-based client collaboration with an engineering team in India to support faster delivery, lower development costs, and 24/7 project progress. Clients get direct business communication in US hours, while our India team extends engineering capacity across software and AI projects.
